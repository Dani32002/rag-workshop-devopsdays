A development sorter exhausted its 1 GiB emptyDir workspace while processing a generated dataset that required about 1.6 GiB of temporary space. Investigation found a copied workspace limit and no ephemeral-storage request, while the node had sufficient capacity. A reviewed manifest provided an approved 2 GiB workspace with compatible pod storage resources and log headroom. The original dataset completed with temporary-file cleanup, and an oversized-input test failed explicitly at its budget rather than consuming unbounded space.
