A preview service emitted metrics but no traces because its Deployment used OTEL_TRACES_EXPORTERS instead of the supported OTEL_TRACES_EXPORTER key. Startup inspection confirmed that the unsupported key left the application's disabled-export default in effect while the collector worked for another service. A reviewed correction enabled export to the approved internal collector without changing sampling. A synthetic trace reached the viewer with the expected service name, and configuration validation now warns clearly about unknown telemetry keys.
