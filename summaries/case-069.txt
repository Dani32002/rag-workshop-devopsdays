A Kubernetes build Job in workshop-build compiled successfully but failed artifact collection because the compiler wrote to /workspace/dist while the collector still searched /workspace/output. Logs and shared-volume checks confirmed an output-path mismatch rather than a mount or permission problem. The reviewed collector path was aligned with the compiler output, and a fresh build produced a package matching the manifest's checksum and build identifier. A new directory-and-package assertion passed normally and failed clearly for deliberately empty output.
