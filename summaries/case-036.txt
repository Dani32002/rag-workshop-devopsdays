Laboratory cross-node file transfers stalled while small and same-node requests succeeded because the overlay retained a 1500-byte MTU on a 1500-byte underlay without allowing for encapsulation. Approved packet-size tests showed failures above the effective overlay limit, and the network owner identified the CNI-supported lower MTU. The change was applied through the lab networking rollout with test-pod recreation as required. Cross-node transfers recovered, regression checks passed, and infrastructure tests now compare overlay and underlay MTU.
