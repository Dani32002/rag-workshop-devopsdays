The cleanup CronJob in the ivy development namespace skipped scheduled runs because its Forbid concurrency policy saw an earlier Job as still active. Logs showed cleanup had completed, but the wrapper waited indefinitely for a background metrics helper with no shutdown path. The wrapper was corrected to stop the helper cleanly and preserve cleanup failure status, and both successful and failing exits were tested. After confirming no cleanup remained active, the owner safely ended the stuck run, and the next scheduled run completed without weakening concurrency protection.
