A laboratory dashboard missed successful Job results because the thirty-second post-completion TTL expired before its two-minute polling cycle. The dashboard depended on pod logs instead of a retained report, so normal cleanup removed its evidence. The test was updated to publish a required report with its synthetic run identifier to managed artifact storage before reporting success, and the dashboard consumed that retained result while keeping bounded Job cleanup. Results remained accessible after deletion, and an artifact-store outage test failed visibly.
