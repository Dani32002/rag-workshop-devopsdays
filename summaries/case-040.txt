A single-replica sandbox file editor rollout stalled with multi-attach errors because a surged replacement on another node shared the old pod's ReadWriteOnce claim. The application did not support concurrent instances and could tolerate a planned sandbox interruption. After owner approval and confirmation that no editing session was active, a reviewed Recreate strategy stopped the old instance before normal volume detach and replacement mounting. Files remained intact without forced detach, and the chart documents the required maintenance window rather than promising uninterrupted service.
