A rehearsal node drain was correctly blocked by a PodDisruptionBudget protecting the only healthy queue-coordinator replica in the juniper namespace. Investigation showed one healthy replica, zero allowed disruptions, and an incorrect ConfigMap reference preventing the other replica from becoming ready. A reviewed correction restored both replicas and allowed one disruption. The normal eviction workflow then moved the pod without interrupting requests, the replacement became ready, and a pre-drain health check was added to the maintenance procedure.
