The staging parser accumulated a queue despite healthy pods and low node-wide CPU usage after its container CPU limit was reduced. Throttling metrics correlated latency bursts with the 100 millicore limit, and a fixed-input benchmark at 500 millicores restored baseline throughput with substantially less throttling. After checking quota and scheduling capacity, the platform owner approved the measured requests and limits for rollout. Queue draining returned to normal with unchanged error rates, and the dashboard now displays throttling alongside usage.
