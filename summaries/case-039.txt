A development archive pod could not mount its existing zonal volume because a diagnostic nodeName pin forced it into zone-b while the PersistentVolume required zone-a. Topology comparison confirmed the conflict, and the obsolete pin had accidentally remained in the template. A reviewed removal let the controller replace the pod and the scheduler select an eligible zone-a node. The existing volume mounted, fixture checksums matched, and template tests now reject unexplained nodeName pinning.
