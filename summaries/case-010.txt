The rehearsal event processor fell behind during a burst because its autoscaler was capped at six replicas. Investigation confirmed the scaling-limited condition and found that quota allowed twelve workers but the existing node pool could accommodate only eight. The platform owner approved temporary nodes within budget, after which a reviewed maximum-replica adjustment allowed scaling to ten and the queue began shrinking at the same input rate. Throughput and temporary capacity were documented, and the agreed test cap was restored after the queue drained.
