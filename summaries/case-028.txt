Pods in the orchid namespace lost Service-name resolution after default-deny egress was introduced without a DNS allowance. Diagnostic queries timed out under the policy but succeeded outside the namespace, while known-address connectivity remained functional. A reviewed narrow rule allowed UDP and TCP port 53 only to the verified cluster DNS namespace and pods. Service lookups and a large-response test succeeded, unrelated external destinations remained denied, and DNS reachability was added to isolation testing.
