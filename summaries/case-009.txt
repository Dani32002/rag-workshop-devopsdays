Staging thumbnail workers in the alder namespace did not autoscale because CPU utilization remained unknown despite visible usage metrics. The HorizontalPodAutoscaler condition and rendered chart showed missing worker CPU requests, which prevented utilization calculations relative to requests. A benchmark-supported 200 millicore request was added through the staging change process after checking quota. Under repeated load the workload scaled from two to five replicas, returned to two after its stabilization window, and gained a chart test requiring requests.
