A short-lived meshed connectivity Job in the dune namespace intermittently failed its first request because the application started before its proxy completed configuration. Request failures consistently preceded the sidecar readiness transition while the destination stayed healthy. The mesh's supported per-workload startup-hold setting delayed application startup until proxy readiness without changing global behavior. Repeated startup tests passed with existing bounded client retries, clean Job termination was verified, and the runner template now records startup and completion requirements.
