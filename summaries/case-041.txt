A test notebook filesystem remained at 10 GiB after its PVC expanded to 20 GiB because this CSI driver required a remount to complete filesystem expansion. The FileSystemResizePending condition, PersistentVolume capacity, and storage-owner confirmation established that backend expansion had already succeeded. During an approved maintenance window, saved notebook work was followed by a controller-managed pod restart. Kubelet resize events reported success, the filesystem showed 20 GiB, and existing fixtures remained readable and intact.
