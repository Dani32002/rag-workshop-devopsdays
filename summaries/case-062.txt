A discovered test exporter was not scraped because its ServiceMonitor endpoint port contained the numeric string 9090 instead of the Service port name telemetry. Diagnostic access and rendered Service inspection confirmed that the exporter listener and port mapping were correct. A reviewed monitor change referenced telemetry, resolving the endpoint and allowing successful scrapes. The /metrics path and thirty-second interval were verified, five consecutive scrapes passed, and the dashboard displayed the expected counters.
