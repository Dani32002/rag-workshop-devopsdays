A rehearsal server-side apply failed because the release manager tried to own a fixed Deployment replica count already managed by the autoscaler. Managed-field inspection confirmed competing responsibilities, and the overlay had not enabled the chart's existing autoscaling-aware omission of replicas. The reviewed render removed that field while retaining autoscaler bounds, allowing apply without forced ownership transfer. Bounded load verified scaling, and the ownership contract and chart test now prevent fixed replicas in this autoscaled overlay.
