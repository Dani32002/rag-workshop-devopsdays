A non-root sandbox processor in the olive namespace could mount its new persistent volume but could not write output because directory ownership did not grant its group 2200 write access. Inspection found a root-owned directory and a missing fsGroup setting, while the CSI driver supported the required ownership behavior. A reviewed securityContext change specified the approved group and the recreated pod received the intended volume access. Output writes succeeded, the process remained non-root, and unrelated directories stayed unwritable.
