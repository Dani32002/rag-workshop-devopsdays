A replacement development PVC could not automatically reuse a retained volume after the old claim was removed during recovery testing. Inspection showed the PersistentVolume was Released with its previous claim identity still referenced, consistent with Retain requiring administrator-led recovery. The storage owner verified the generated dataset and performed documented ownership reconciliation and prebinding. The replacement claim bound to the verified volume, fixture validation passed before writes resumed, and the runbook now distinguishes retention from automatic reuse.
