The rehearsal load balancer included a worker without a local gateway endpoint despite the Service using externalTrafficPolicy=Local to preserve source addresses. Investigation showed its static backend list and generic node health check did not reflect Service-specific local endpoint availability. The owner configured the supported Service health-check integration, excluding nodes without local backends. A controlled replica move confirmed endpoint-aware backend eligibility and uninterrupted routing through healthy nodes, while source-address behavior remained intact and Local was retained.
