A development VerticalPodAutoscaler produced larger resource recommendations without changing pods because update mode Off intentionally provided recommendations only. Checking the installed behavior confirmed that automatic mutation or eviction was not expected for this non-disruptive sizing exercise. The recommendation was compared with measured peaks and namespace capacity before an owner-approved chart update applied the measured request through the normal release process. Recommendation-only mode remained enabled, and the lesson now explains that recommendations do not themselves mutate pods.
