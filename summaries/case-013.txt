Integration agents in the hazel namespace could not schedule after a node-pool replacement because required affinity still referenced pool-generation=old. Comparison with replacement-node labels showed pool-generation=current, and investigation established that the copied generation constraint was not intended to define workload eligibility. A reviewed template change used the stable workload-pool=integration label while preserving integration-only placement. Replacement agents scheduled successfully and passed a representative integration task without depending on temporary generation labels.
