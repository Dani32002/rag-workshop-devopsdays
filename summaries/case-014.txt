The third rehearsal gateway replica remained pending because strict topology spreading required three domains while only two eligible zones had nodes. Scheduler events confirmed a spread conflict, and infrastructure review found that the third-zone pool had been omitted. Because the rehearsal required three-zone availability, the approved correction restored the missing pool rather than weakening DoNotSchedule constraints. The replica then scheduled in the third zone, distribution reached one gateway per zone, and the controlled zone-loss test passed.
