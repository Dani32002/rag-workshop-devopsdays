A synthetic workspace resource remained stuck deleting because its finalizer-owning cleanup controller had been scaled to zero before removing the generated external directory. Ownership and mock-service access checks confirmed the controller could still perform its cleanup contract. Restoring one replica through the lab reset workflow allowed it to delete and verify the directory, remove its own finalizer, and complete resource deletion. The reset sequence now waits for owned-resource cleanup before stopping the controller, and a repeat test left no stuck resources.
