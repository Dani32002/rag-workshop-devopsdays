A 10 GiB development PVC in the pebble namespace remained Pending because its StorageClass intentionally used WaitForFirstConsumer and no pod referenced it. Claim events showed waiting rather than provisioning failure, confirming expected delayed binding behavior. A reviewed disposable consumer pod allowed the scheduler to choose a node and storage provisioning to use its zone. The claim bound, the pod mounted it, and a generated file round trip passed, with delayed binding explained in the exercise instructions.
