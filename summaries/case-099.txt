Rehearsal controller replicas could not elect a leader because omitted configuration sent their Lease operations to default, outside their approved rehearsal-control RBAC scope. Error inspection confirmed Forbidden responses and identified the application's fallback after chart refactoring. A reviewed explicit namespace setting made both replicas use the same Lease in rehearsal-control without expanding permissions. Tests verified exactly one leader, controlled failover after lease expiry, and clear startup failure when the required namespace setting was missing.
