Lower-priority generated batch tasks were preempted during an availability rehearsal when an approved higher-priority gateway needed resources on a full pool. Events, PriorityClasses, and the rehearsal design confirmed that disposable batch work was intended to yield. Checkpoints preserved completed work and supported idempotent retries, so remaining tasks resumed in a later window rather than raising their priority. Final item counts matched without missing or duplicate work, and the exercise now documents expected preemption and recovery.
