A sandbox pod could not mount its bound claim on a new storage worker because the CSI driver was not registered there. Investigation found that the node-plugin DaemonSet excluded the worker because it lacked a toleration for the new dedicated storage taint. After confirming driver support, the storage owner added the narrow approved toleration through the managed driver deployment. The plugin registered the driver, the pod's mount retry succeeded, and a generated file round trip passed without altering the claim.
