The rehearsal gateway interrupted long generated export requests during rollouts because its thirty-second termination grace period was shorter than the forty-second handler duration. Shutdown logs confirmed grace expiry, and measurements also identified an ingress propagation window before new traffic stopped. A reviewed seventy-five-second total budget accounted for bounded application draining and any preStop time within the shared grace period. Repeated rollout tests let long requests finish while deliberately stalled requests still terminated within the configured bound.
